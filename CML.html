Core AI Capabilities for
Legal Professionals

We don't just store documents; we actively analyze, draft, and protect your legal assets across their entire lifecycle.

Agentic Contract Drafting & Optimization

Context-aware generative drafting built securely within an in-app editor. LEXI utilizes your historical playbook data to provide instant term optimization, eliminating manual data entry, version control chaos, and disjointed email requests.

Automated Bulk Assessment & Redlining

Seamlessly reconcile Master Service Agreements (MSAs) and hundreds of amendments using precise "Contract Coordinates." When counterparties submit redlines, LEXI instantly cross-references deviations against your approved legal playbook, suggesting compliant fallback clauses.

Pre-Computation Smart Redaction

Security before processing. Sensitive financial data, account numbers, and PII are automatically detected and permanently masked before any foundational AI processing occurs, guaranteeing strict compliance with CPRA, GDPR, and HIPAA.

In-Platform Secure Legal Collaboration

Maintain an unbroken audit trail. Utilize native WebRTC video conferencing to negotiate directly within the secure document environment, eliminating the security risks of third-party video apps and fragmented chat threads.

Proactive Risk Insights & Tracking

LEXI extracts key dates, payment milestones, and strict penalty clauses, pushing automated alerts to your legal team before a breach occurs or a critical renewal window closes.
